🌾 About Us

Ampal Agro Chemicals Private Limited

Ampal Agro Chemicals Private Limited is a trusted name in the agricultural industry, committed to empowering farmers with high-quality products, innovative solutions, and reliable support. With a strong belief in sustainable farming and modern agri-practices, we work closely with farmers to help them achieve better yields, improved soil health, and long-term prosperity.

Founded with a vision to bring science-backed, result-oriented agricultural products to every farm, AMPAL AGRO has steadily grown into a brand known for quality, transparency, and farmer-focused service. Our wide range of products—spanning fertilizers, seeds, soil conditioners, micronutrients, organic solutions, crop protectants, and speciality agri-inputs—are developed to meet the diverse needs of Indian agriculture.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To increase crop productivity, improve farmers’ income, and contribute to long-term agricultural development by becoming a reliable and innovative company that provides sustainable and effective agricultural solutions based on scientific research.

Our Mission

To develop and make available high-quality biostimulants, specialty fertilizers, and micronutrients, to provide farmers with reliable, field-proven solutions and technical guidance, to maintain the highest levels of quality, safety, and regulatory standards, and to promote sustainable and responsible agricultural practices.

Our Journey

From humble beginnings to becoming leaders in agricultural solutions.

2009 - Shri Mahalaxmi Agro Services

Founded with a deep understanding of crop nutrition and trust, we began by serving local farmers, establishing lasting relationships within the farming community.

2021 - SM Agro

We expanded by launching SM Agro, offering biostimulant solutions designed to enhance crop resilience. These products received widespread market acceptance.

2025 - Ampal Agro Chemicals Pvt. Ltd.

To meet the growing demand for specialty fertilizers, we established Ampal Agro Chemicals Pvt. Ltd. in 2025, focusing on high-quality products and sustainable practices.

Looking Forward

Guided by innovation and integrity, we continue to deliver reliable, high-performance crop solutions that empower farmers and contribute to long-term agricultural growth.
